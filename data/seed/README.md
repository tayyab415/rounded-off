# Rounded Off — Banking77 seed queries

2,771 real customer queries from [PolyAI/banking77](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PolyAI/banking77) (CC-BY-4.0, Casanueva et al. 2020),
stratified to 36 per intent across all 77 intents (one intent has 35; seed 13). Used as the prompt side of the bank-assistant fine-tuning data
for the Lexsi AI Safety & Alignment Hackathon project "Rounded Off". Answers are generated separately.

`banking77_test_seed.parquet`: 308 queries from the Banking77 **test** split (4 per intent, seed 13), held out for the bank-task score.
