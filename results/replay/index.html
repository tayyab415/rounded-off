BankDesk replay: the same request, played by two models

BankDesk is a bank we simulate in code. The model plays the support agent: it sees the customer's account, can check the payee and the session, ask the customer questions, and send money. Each scenario card gives two cases that open with the same message, word for word: in one a scammer is coaching the customer, in the other the customer is genuine. Code, not an LLM judge, scores where the money went.

On 111 held-out test cards, R_wise50 sent money to the scammer in 94.8% of English and 83.0% of Hindi scam cases. GRPO3 did so in 0.7% and 2.3%, and completed 96.3% and 97.7% of genuine requests.

  • R_wise50: Tiny Aya Global fine-tuned as an English and Hindi bank assistant, then safety-repaired. The model a bank following best practice would ship.
  • GRPO3: R_wise50 trained on BankDesk: SFT on teacher demonstrations, then GRPO with a reward computed by code.

How this page was made

  • Episodes: GRPO3 from results/raw/hf_runs/bankdesk/e374042/GRPO3.default.jsonl, R_wise50 from results/raw/hf_runs/bankdesk/311e670/R_wise50.default.jsonl. Both are BankDesk v3 runs on the same 444 test cases (111 cards in data/bankdesk/cards_v3.jsonl), with normal bank checks and a GLM-5.3-Flash customer who answers from the card's hidden story. These runs give the numbers in results/bankdesk_v3/FINAL_TEST.md.
  • Card choice, by rule: R_wise50 sent money to the scammer in case A, GRPO3 asked the customer a question before stopping in case A, and GRPO3 completed the payment in case B. 62 of 67 English and 38 of 44 Hindi test cards meet it. Among those we prefer no rejected actions, GRPO3 asking in case B too, a Hindi card from a different scam family than the English one, the fewest turns, then the closest wording between GRPO3's two questions.
  • Bank checks are generated by code with noise on purpose, so no single check settles a case.
  • Model and customer text is verbatim, except that Markdown asterisks are removed and the harness's "actions left" counter is dropped. The English lines under Hindi text are our translation, not model output.
  • Built by scripts/bankdesk_replay.py. No scripts, fonts or files load from the network.
